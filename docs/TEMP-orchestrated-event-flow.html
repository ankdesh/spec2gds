Keep the evidence separate

Model review

review.ready establishes reviewed ancestry. Its result is prose; it does not grant human architecture approval or prove a service save.

Human decision

Required architecture approval is a separate decision. Restricted Spec content is subject to Spec Editor approval under the accepted contract.

Service receipt

An exact applied receipt and revision prove saved Spec content. Circuit sent proves handoff; accepted draft and saved source are later outcomes.

What the current runtime does after restart

Active execution becomes needs-resume. Dispatching or accepted deliveries become interrupted. Resume retains budgets. An uncertain attempt also needs explicit acknowledgement before retry. Stop is terminal and does not undo a saved document.

What event subscriptions do not provide

The stock chain is an exact-type reaction chain. It has no general dependency scheduler, joins, subscription payload filters, or typed execution-stage mappings. The stock templates also have no general subscription from review.applied back to the primary. A declared outcome event is not a verified completion loop.

How this example differs from the product

Each click applies a prepared illustrative transition. The browser does not run Harness or validate a real Spec. Example receipt IDs are invented teaching data. The admission calculation follows the inspected latest-input ancestry guard. Human decisions, Circuit draft acceptance, and source save are shown as separate actions. Current contract gaps are listed on the readiness page.
